  3. Har bir tur uchun "Create new product" ni bosing
    Pastdagi har bir maydon yonida "Nusxa" tugmasi bor. Bosing va GetYourGuide'dagi tegishli joyga qo'ying (Ctrl+V).
  4. Rasmlarni yuklang
    Har bir turga kamida 6 ta rasm: yotiq (gorizontal), kamida 1280 piksel eni, ustida yozuv, logotip yoki suv belgisi bo'lmasin. Sun'iy intellekt yasagan rasm mumkin emas. Eng yaxshisi - o'z turlaringizdan haqiqiy suratlar.
  5. Narxni kiriting
    Har bir turda narx jadvali bor. GetYourGuide'da "Pricing" bo'limida narxni "per person" va guruh kattaligi bo'yicha (participant tiers) kiriting: "GetYourGuide narxi" ustunidagi raqamlarni qo'ying. Bu raqamlar sizning narxingizga 25% qo'shilgan holda hisoblangan.
  6. Tekshiruvga yuboring
    GetYourGuide har bir turni bir necha kun ichida tekshiradi va o'zgartirish so'rashi mumkin. Shunda menga yuboring - tuzatib beraman.

Full description (to'liq tavsif)
Plov is the dish of Uzbek celebrations, and cooking it is a ritual.

Your local cook (oshpaz) meets you at the Siyob Bazaar, where you choose the rice, yellow carrots and lamb by sight and smell. In the kitchen you cut, fry and layer everything in a large kazan, learning the secrets of the zirvak and the steam while the smell fills the courtyard. While the plov rests, you make a fresh tomato and onion salad.

Then everyone sits down at the dastarkhan to taste the plov you cooked, with fresh bread and green tea. You leave with a take-away portion and a printed recipe card.
